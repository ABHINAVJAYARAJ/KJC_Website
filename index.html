We bring academic perspectives to practical business questions, with a focus on clear scopes, useful research and recommendations that organisations can act on.

04 / Our network

Connected through
Junior Enterprises Europe.

KJC is part of the Junior Enterprise model and recognised within Junior Enterprises Europe. That gives us a wider frame of reference than a standalone student society, with shared practices, peer benchmarking and a recognised European network behind the model.

Junior Enterprises Europe

The European federation for Junior Enterprises, founded in 1992 and based in Brussels.

What JEE is

Junior Enterprises Europe represents student-led consultancies across Europe. It provides a recognised framework for how Junior Enterprises operate, collaborate and develop.

Why it matters to clients

  • A proven model for structured, scoped student consulting.
  • Exposure to shared practices shaped by work across Europe.
  • Fresh thinking grounded in academic rigour and practical delivery.

What it means for KJC

  • Stronger governance and a clearer operating model.
  • Benchmarking against other Junior Enterprises across Europe.
  • Added credibility as part of a recognised network.
